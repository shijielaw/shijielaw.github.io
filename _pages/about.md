I'm a PhD student in Software Engineering from [School of Computer Science and Technology](https://cs.tongji.edu.cn/), [Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/). Now my main research interest includes large language models, knowledge graph reasoning and reinforcement learning. In master period, my research focused on social networks, graph neural networks and group link prediction.
